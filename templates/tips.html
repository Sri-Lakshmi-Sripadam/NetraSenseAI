💡 Eye Care Tips

7 science-backed habits to protect your vision • Recommended by ophthalmologists • Follow daily for healthy eyes

👁️ 7 Expert Tips🧠 Doctor Approved⏱️ Daily 5 Min🛡️ Prevent 80% Issues
👀

Follow the 20-20-20 Rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Reduces digital eye strain by 60% instantly.

💻 For Screen Users
💧

Blink Frequently

We blink 50% less on screens! Blink 15-20 times per minute. Helps prevent dry eyes, irritation & blurry vision.

💧 Prevents Dryness
🥕

Eat Healthy Foods

Carrots, spinach, fish, eggs, citrus fruits & nuts are rich in Vitamin A, C, Omega-3. Boosts retina health naturally.

🥗 Nutrition
😴

Get Enough Sleep

7-8 hours sleep allows your eyes to rest, repair & stay hydrated. Reduces dark circles, strain & redness.

😴 Recovery
🕶

Protect Your Eyes

Wear UV-protected sunglasses outdoors. UV rays cause cataract & macular damage. Even on cloudy days!

☀️ UV Protection
💻

Maintain Proper Screen Distance

Keep monitor 50-70 cm away and slightly below eye level. Top of screen at or below eye level reduces neck + eye strain.

📏 Ergonomics
👨‍⚕️

Regular Eye Check-ups

Visit an ophthalmologist yearly, especially if you notice vision changes. Early detection prevents 80% of blindness!
